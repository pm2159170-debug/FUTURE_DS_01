A professional, interactive sales analytics dashboard analysing retail business performance across products, categories, regions, and time.

---

## 🔍 Project Overview

This project analyses a retail superstore's sales data to answer key business questions:

- Which products generate the most revenue?
- How do sales change over time (monthly/quarterly)?
- Which categories and regions are most profitable?
- Where should the business focus to grow faster?

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- 💰 **Total Revenue:** $2.33M in FY2024 — up **14.2%** from the previous year
- 🏆 **Top Category:** Technology drives **36%** of revenue with the highest profit margins
- 📅 **Peak Month:** November is the strongest sales month (Q4 surge)
- 🌍 **Best Region:** West region leads all four quarters consistently
- ⚠️ **Problem Area:** Tables sub-category runs at **−8% margin** and needs urgent review

---

## ✅ Strategic Recommendations

1. **Double down on Technology** — highest margins, strong Q4 momentum
2. **Fix the Tables sub-category** — the only loss-making product line
3. **Invest in the Central region** — lags West by 31% despite similar market size
